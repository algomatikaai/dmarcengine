## 🎯 Business Overview

### Mission
Build the world's first automated DMARC management platform that turns DIY email authentication into professional-grade protection.

### Market Opportunity
- **Target Market:** 42.7M companies with DIY DMARC implementations
- **Market Gap:** 98% of DMARC users are underserved by enterprise solutions
- **Competitive Advantage:** First to serve SMB market with $49-149/month pricing

### Business Model
- **Revenue:** Monthly recurring subscriptions
- **Pricing:** Starter ($49), Professional ($99), Business ($149)
- **Acquisition:** Automated outreach and content marketing
- **Unit Economics:** $11.78 CAC, $2,640 LTV, 224:1 LTV/CAC ratio
